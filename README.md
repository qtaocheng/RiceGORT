# RiceGORT
The input parameters, measured canopy spectra, and implementation codes of the PROSAIL, Row4SAIL, and RiceGORT.

PROSAIL assumes a homogeneous and continuous canopy, whereas Row4SAIL and RiceGORT represent the rice canopy as a discontinuous canopy structure. Our modifications to the radiative transfer model go beyond simple parameterization and involve two key aspects: (i) radiative transfer modeling of the rice canopy over ridge-top areas, and (ii) integration of rice canopy scene reflectance by introducing a geometric–optical model.
